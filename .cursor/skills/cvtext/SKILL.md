# CV details

`cvtext.txt` at the project root is the reference for full details.

When a work or education item is opened or updated, show every bullet from that entry in `cvtext.txt`. Do not replace those bullets with a one-line summary. Keep the title, organization, and dates aligned with that file.

The timeline row stays short. The click card carries the full list.

Education entries that have no bullets in `cvtext.txt` stay as the degree, school, and dates only. Do not add the GCE rows to the timeline unless the user asks for them.
